About us

A local business built on quality, trust and community

We started DOK Prime Ventures to give our community products and services they can rely on, and to create opportunities along the way.

Our story

How DOK Prime Ventures began

DOK Prime Ventures was founded by Victoria Dok with a clear idea: everyday customers deserve quality products and honest, dependable service. What began as [how the business started, e.g. a small poultry farm] has grown into three connected businesses serving households, farmers and businesses.

Today we raise poultry and supply fresh eggs and birds, sell authentic smocks and traditional clothing, and run a mobile money (MoMo) point that helps people send, receive and manage money with ease.

Different products, one promise: quality you can see, service you can trust, and a business that gives back to the community it serves.
