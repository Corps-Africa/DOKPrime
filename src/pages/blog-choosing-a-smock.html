How to choose the right smock for your occasion

A well-made smock is more than clothing. It carries tradition, craft and pride. Here's how to pick one that fits the moment and fits you well.

1. Start with the occasion

Think about where you'll wear it. For weddings, festivals and ceremonies, many people choose richer colours and fuller styles. For church, work or everyday wear, a simpler pattern and lighter style is often more comfortable.

2. Choose your colours

Smock cloth is woven in strips, so the colours and stripes give each piece its character. Bold colours stand out at celebrations; classic combinations go with almost anything. If you're dressing as a group, agree on one or two colours so everyone matches.

3. Get the size right

A smock should sit comfortably on the shoulders and allow easy movement. When you order, send us your usual shirt size or chest measurement, and tell us whether you like a looser or neater fit. We'll help you choose.

4. Think about the full look

Many smocks are worn with matching trousers or a hat. Ask us about complete outfits if you want everything to match.

5. Order early for big events

For weddings, funerals and festivals, especially group orders, contact us as early as possible so we can get the right colours and sizes ready for you.
