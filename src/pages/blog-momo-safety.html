5 simple ways to stay safe with mobile money

Mobile money makes life easier, but fraudsters know that too. These five habits will help keep your money where it belongs: with you.

  1. Never share your PIN. Not with friends, not with callers, not even with a MoMo agent. No genuine agent or network staff will ever ask for it.
  2. Check the name before you confirm. When sending money, read the recipient's name on your screen carefully before you approve the transaction.
  3. Be careful with "wrong transfer" calls and messages. If someone says they sent money to you by mistake, check your balance and official transaction messages first. Don't send anything back until you've confirmed it with your network.
  4. Keep your transaction messages. Your confirmation SMS and transaction ID are your proof if anything goes wrong.
  5. Use agents you trust. Transact at a known, registered MoMo point and ask for help if anything looks unusual.
